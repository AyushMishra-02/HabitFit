Beginner Training

4-week beginner workout plan

Use this plan if you want to start exercising but do not want a complicated routine. You will train three days per week, walk on two optional days, and keep two days flexible for rest.

Weekly schedule

DaySessionTime
MondayFull-body strength A25-30 min
TuesdayEasy walk or rest15-25 min
WednesdayMobility and core20 min
ThursdayRestFlexible
FridayFull-body strength B25-35 min
WeekendWalk, stretch, or restFlexible

Strength A

  1. Chair squat: 2-3 sets of 8-10 reps
  2. Incline push-up: 2-3 sets of 6-10 reps
  3. Glute bridge: 2-3 sets of 10-12 reps
  4. One-arm backpack row: 2 sets of 8 each side
  5. Dead bug: 2 sets of 6 each side

Strength B

  1. Reverse lunge or step-back tap: 2 sets of 6 each side
  2. Dumbbell or backpack deadlift: 2-3 sets of 8-10 reps
  3. Wall press or incline push-up: 2 sets of 8 reps
  4. Side plank from knees: 2 sets of 15-25 seconds each side
  5. Farmer carry: 3 walks of 20-30 seconds

How to progress

When every set feels controlled, add one or two reps next time. Once you reach the top of the range, add a little resistance or slow the lowering phase. Keep at least one or two reps in reserve so the habit stays repeatable.

Quick checklist

  • Warm up for five minutes.
  • Rest 45-90 seconds between sets.
  • Stop sharp pain immediately.
  • Track how the session felt.
