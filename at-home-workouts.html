At-home workouts for beginners

These workouts use bodyweight, a chair, a wall, and a small amount of floor space. Keep the first two weeks easy enough that you finish feeling better than when you started.

20-minute no-equipment session

  1. March in place: 2 minutes
  2. Sit-to-stand squat: 3 rounds of 8 reps
  3. Wall push-up: 3 rounds of 8 reps
  4. Standing hip hinge: 3 rounds of 10 reps
  5. Bird dog: 2 rounds of 6 each side
  6. Easy walk around the room: 2 minutes

10-minute mobility reset

Move slowly through cat-cow, hip flexor stretch, hamstring reach, chest opener, child's pose, and nasal breathing. Use this on rest days or after a strength session.

Low-impact cardio options

Walk outside, climb stairs slowly, do step taps, or cycle at an easy pace. Beginners should be able to speak in short sentences during low-impact cardio.

Make it easier

  • Use a higher surface for push-ups.
  • Reduce reps before reducing form quality.
  • Pause the timer whenever you need to breathe.
