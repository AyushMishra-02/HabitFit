Food Basics

Nutrition for fitness beginners

Nutrition supports training; it does not have to take over your life. Start with regular meals, enough protein, and a simple plan for busy days.

Beginner plate method

Build meals around one palm of protein, one fist of carbohydrates, two fists of vegetables or fruit, and a small serving of fat. Adjust portions based on appetite, goals, and energy.

Easy workout snacks

  • Banana with yogurt
  • Toast with eggs
  • Oats with milk and berries
  • Rice bowl with beans or chicken
  • Smoothie with fruit and protein

Hydration

Keep water nearby and drink regularly through the day. For short beginner sessions, water is enough for most people.

Recovery meals

After training, aim for a familiar meal that includes protein and carbohydrates. A rice bowl, eggs and toast, dal and rice, or yogurt with fruit can all work.

Simple rule

Do not chase perfect eating. Build meals you can repeat on a normal weekday.
